## The entry

`main.py` writes one single-line joke about office life to
`$MAKEFUN_OUTPUT_DIR/entry.txt` (falling back to `/output/entry.txt`):

> The meeting that could have been an email ended with a follow-up meeting to schedule another meeting about why it couldn't have been an email.

The joke has a setup (the meeting that could have been an email) and a
turn (a follow-up meeting to schedule yet another meeting about it), built
on something anyone who has worked in an office recognises, with the
punchline last — 142 characters, one line, UTF-8.

## Run it

```sh
MAKEFUN_OUTPUT_DIR=/tmp/out python3 main.py
cat /tmp/out/entry.txt
```
